# foundations-canonical — synthesis

Practical-first synthesis of the 100 claims in this bucket. Every assertion
is pinned to SC-* ids. Verdicts come later, in campaigns; this file organizes
the art, it does not adjudicate it.

Bucket composition: AR01 (76), SI01 (15), SI02 (2), SI05 (2), TC03 (2),
SI07 (1), TC01 (1), TC02 (1). AR01 is the bi-wave least-action paper; SI01 is
the canonical-methods site text whose "gas cluster" section carries the
bucket's only substantial practical content (ring currents, current
crowding, charge bubbles). Several SI01/TC02/TC03 claims have sibling claims
from the same documents sitting in the `electrical-devices` bucket
(e.g. SI01-018/019/021, TC04-016, PA01-001/003/005) — flagged inline below
where the practical content is device-flavored.

## The art (practical layer)

- **Ampère–Foucault ring currents** (the bucket's most device-relevant
  family; siblings in `electrical-devices` and `general`):
  - Excitation: ring currents of atomic and subatomic diameters can be
    excited in a conductor by an alternating magnetic field [SC-SI01-030].
  - Geometry: ring-current diameters an order of magnitude below 1 nm,
    approaching the first Bohr orbit of hydrogen [SC-SI01-031].
  - Thermal signature: claimed zero heat release — electrons orbit without
    colliding with thermally moving ions, held by electric-field pressure
    instead of the Coulomb force; lifetime exceeding 100 years (possibly
    forever) [SC-SI01-032].
  - Measured/calculated resistivity: 10^-15 to 10^-20 Ohm·m, against copper
    at 16 nOhm·m [SC-SI01-033].
  - Application claim: high-temperature superconductivity exists only for
    ring-current geometry; enables sub-nano computers and ultra-low-power
    devices [SC-SI01-034].
  - Quantitative siblings live cross-bucket in `general`: ring-current
    diameters in steel at room temperature "orders of magnitude below
    0.1 nm" (SC-SI09-006), permanent magnets as room-temperature
    ring-current superconductors (SC-SI09-007, SC-TC04-018), and the
    steel-plate hardware (SC-TC04-017…TC04-026).
- **AC current distribution over a conductor cross-section**: four variants
  claimed where one was believed [SC-SI01-023]; in cylindrical symmetry at
  least three solutions including an axially compressed "cord" that
  theorists missed — identified by Kuh & Ibrahim (2001) in experiments and
  named the Current Crowding Effect [SC-SI01-024]. Configurations are
  claimed to be described by the Maxwell system plus the differential Ohm
  law [SC-SI01-040]; at equality of current and field pressures the current
  can be thrown from the wall boundaries into the conductor interior,
  forming a planar current [SC-SI01-041].
- **Charge bubbles as observed objects**: space-charge waves (SCW) in
  current-carrying conductors claimed to be a periodic sequence of electron
  charge bubbles; bead lightning a sequence of plasmoid bubbles
  [SC-SI01-015]; a thin-wall charge bubble arises on the surface of a
  charged metal sphere with bubble radius equal to the sphere radius, and
  the pressure gradient of the field holds like charges from dispersal (the
  claimed resolution of the electrostatics paradox) [SC-SI01-012; sibling
  SC-WO04-025 in `emden-gravity-cosmic`].
- **Over-unity claim anchoring this path**: processes of alternating
  displacement-current generation "do not obey the energy conservation
  laws" [SC-SI01-020] — the assertion on which the `general`-bucket CODM/
  capacitive-transformer family (SC-TC02-*, SC-TC03-*, SC-SI09-005) is
  built; enter those campaigns via this pin. The bucket's own experimental
  anchor: displacement currents with gigahertz frequencies effectively burn
  the conductors of microchips onto whose lateral surface the fields
  generated by these currents fall [SC-TC02-011].
- **Planar inductance patent** [SC-SI07-003]: RU 2614 188 C1 claims the
  technical result of increasing the numerical L values of a planar
  inductance (≥1 turn on an insulating substrate; HF/microwave amplifiers,
  mixers, RLC filters). Design parameters of the patent family are
  extracted in `general` (SC-PA01-002, SC-PA02-002/003/004, SC-PA03-002…009).
- **What the theory predicts observably (practical-first)**: (i) a second
  diffraction-relevant wave exists but is practically invisible at
  nonrelativistic energies because the two wavelengths differ by several
  orders of magnitude — any bi-wave electron-diffraction test must use
  relativistic electrons [SC-AR01-020]; (ii) the natural diffraction barrier
  for such a test is a slit "tens of picometers wide", and the source
  states it is unclear how to build one [SC-AR01-075]; (iii) the
  quasi-classical quantization conditions for electrons in a central field
  must use a velocity-contracted longitudinal electron size (rest value =
  Compton wavelength) [SC-AR01-071]; (iv) ring currents should show zero
  calorimetric heat and extreme resistivity when individually addressed
  [SC-SI01-032, SC-SI01-033].

## Testable protocols

- **Bi-wave electron diffraction search** [SC-AR01-020, SC-AR01-072,
  SC-AR01-075, SC-AR01-071]. (1) The two action waves coincide only at
  low velocity with λk = Compton wavelength; they differ by orders of
  magnitude at nonrelativistic energies, so standard diffraction cannot
  separate them [SC-AR01-020]. (2) The stated discriminating regime is
  relativistic electrons (wavelengths converge as u → 1, both scales carry
  √(1−u²) contraction [SC-AR01-065, SC-AR01-069]). (3) Barrier: slit of
  tens of picometers [SC-AR01-075]. (4) Compare diffraction pattern against
  single-wave prediction; the theory also demands Compton-scale longitudinal
  electron size enter the quantization conditions [SC-AR01-071].
  [GAP]: fabrication of the pm-scale natural barrier is explicitly flagged
  as unsolved by the source; no relativistic diffraction setup is extracted
  anywhere in the corpus.
- **Ring-current excitation + calorimetry** [SC-SI01-030, SC-SI01-031,
  SC-SI01-032, SC-SI01-033]. (1) Drive an alternating magnetic field around
  a conductor to excite ring currents [SC-SI01-030]. (2) Look for the
  claimed null calorimetric signature [SC-SI01-032]. (3) Bound resistivity
  against the calculated 10^-15…10^-20 Ohm·m [SC-SI01-033]. (4) Cross-check
  geometry (diameter an order below 1 nm) against any magnetic-moment or
  cryogenic observable [SC-SI01-031]. [GAP]: coil geometry, frequencies and
  current amplitudes for the excitation are not extracted in this bucket;
  nearest concrete hardware (steel plates, 500 A pulse) is in `general`
  (SC-TC04-023).
- **Current-crowding / four-variant AC distribution map** [SC-SI01-023,
  SC-SI01-024, SC-SI01-040, SC-SI01-041]. (1) Solve the Maxwell + differential
  Ohm system for a cylindrical conductor [SC-SI01-040]; enumerate the
  claimed ≥3 transverse solutions including the axial cord [SC-SI01-024].
  (2) Measure the transverse AC current distribution in a conductor at
  conditions where crowding is reported (Kuh & Ibrahim 2001 is the pinned
  experimental anchor). (3) Test the equal-pressure interior ejection →
  planar current prediction [SC-SI01-041]. [GAP]: conductor dimensions,
  frequencies and the probing technique are not extracted.
- **Zitterbewegung period vs velocity** [SC-AR01-065, SC-AR01-066].
  The trembling period is claimed to CONTRACT with speed: Tb = T0√(1−u²)
  [SC-AR01-065, SC-AR01-066]. Any measurement that fixes the transverse
  oscillation period of a moving electron (e.g. via channeling radiation or
  storage-ring decoherence) would address this. [GAP]: no measurement
  technique for Tb is extracted; protocol is a naming of the target
  observable, not a procedure.

## Phenomenology map

- **What the theory predicts observably (practical-first).** (i) Every
  moving massive charge carries TWO plane action waves: the temporal wave =
  de Broglie wave with superluminal phase velocity, and the coordinate wave
  with subluminal phase velocity whose wavelength equals the Compton
  wavelength at small velocities [SC-AR01-003, SC-AR01-017, SC-SI02-008,
  SC-SI05-011]; electron diffraction experiments have not seen the second
  wave because the wavelengths differ by orders of magnitude [SC-AR01-020].
  (ii) Corpuscle-wave dualism is deepened to "corpuscular-bi-wave dualism"
  with velocity-dependent scales in every inertial frame [SC-AR01-022].
  (iii) A resting charge is a "trembling string" segment in action space,
  bounded by the Compton wavelength [SC-AR01-005, SC-SI02-009]; its
  trembling period contracts with velocity [SC-AR01-066] and its
  longitudinal size contracts as λ0√(1−u²) [SC-AR01-070, SC-AR01-071].
  (iv) The speed-of-light limit is attributed to a resonance between the
  two waves as u → 1 [SC-AR01-024]. (v) In conductors: SCWs are bubble
  sequences, bead lightning is plasmoid sequences [SC-SI01-015]; current
  re-distributes into cords/planar sheets under field pressure
  [SC-SI01-024, SC-SI01-041]; ring currents are permanently persistent,
  heat-free, superconducting [SC-SI01-032, SC-SI01-034].
- **Thresholds.** Bi-wave description applies only for motion over
  distances exceeding the Compton wavelength [SC-AR01-072]; nonrelativistic
  mechanics needs only the de Broglie wave (least-action = least-time),
  relativistic motion needs only the Compton wave [SC-AR01-023]. Phase
  degeneracy: a plane wave with ω < kc degenerates in frame K1 moving at
  V = ω/k; with ω > kc in frame K2 at V = kc²/ω; no degeneracy at ω = kc
  [SC-AR01-006, SC-AR01-007, SC-AR01-008]; the free-charge action itself
  has the two degeneracy forms SE′ = −t′, S′p = −x′ in the rest frame
  [SC-AR01-061, SC-AR01-067]; the nonrelativistic action S = pr − Et does
  NOT possess degeneracy [SC-AR01-063].
- **Conservation-law structure (claimed observable consequences).** All
  three components p + A are conserved only in homogeneous non-static
  fields [SC-AR01-014]; inhomogeneous static fields conserve energy,
  time-only-dependent fields conserve full momentum — a claimed time/
  coordinate symmetry [SC-AR01-015, SC-AR01-029, SC-AR01-030, SC-AR01-031];
  a charge in a homogeneous variable electric field moves rectilinearly,
  like light in a medium with time-only-dependent refractive index
  [SC-AR01-059, SC-AR01-056, SC-AR01-057, SC-AR01-058].
- **Single-wave quantum mechanics** is called an "unfinished theoretical
  dogma"; a relativistic bi-wave mechanics is proposed as the completion
  [SC-SI05-012].

## Open questions for campaigns

1. Under what exact conditions (energy, barrier) do the two action waves
   give a separable diffraction signature, given λb/λk = 1/u [SC-AR01-020,
   SC-AR01-065, SC-AR01-069]? Does any existing relativistic-electron
   interference dataset already bound the effect?
2. What calculation produces ring-current resistivity 10^-15…10^-20 Ohm·m
   [SC-SI01-033], and what measured quantity (calorimetric null, persistent
   current decay, magnetic moment) could falsify it at the claimed >100-year
   lifetime [SC-SI01-032]?
3. Do the claimed four AC cross-section distributions [SC-SI01-023] and ≥3
   cylindrical solutions [SC-SI01-024] reproduce from Maxwell + differential
   Ohm as stated [SC-SI01-040], and does the "cord" solution coincide with
   the published Current Crowding Effect?
4. Is the equal-pressure ejection of current into the conductor interior
   [SC-SI01-041] observed in any extracted dataset, and at what pressure
   threshold?
5. Does the trembling-period contraction Tb = T0√(1−u²) [SC-AR01-066] leave
   any signature in channeling radiation or beam coherence that campaigns
   can check against published data?
6. The claimed resonance origin of the c-limit [SC-AR01-024] — is it a
   derivation or a metaphor? What would measure the "resonance"?
7. Patent RU 2614 188 C1 [SC-SI07-003]: what measured L values substantiate
   the claimed increase, and how do they relate to the PA01–PA03 parameter
   grids in `general` (Ki, Ky, film thickness h2)?
8. Are SCWs as charge-bubble sequences [SC-SI01-015] reconcilable with the
   SCW velocity/wavelength data in the electrical literature the corpus has
   not yet sourced?
