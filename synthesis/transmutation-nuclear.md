# transmutation-nuclear — synthesis

Practical-first synthesis of the 131 claims in this bucket. Every assertion
is pinned to SC-* ids. This file organizes
the art, it does not adjudicate it. The dominant source is AR04 (proton
charge clusters of nuclear size, 37 claims), flanked by the Shoulders EV/NEV
appendix (MO02), the monograph energy balances (MO03), the E-CAT/ХТЯ analysis
(TC08), and the works-level syntheses (WO01, WO04). SI02 duplicates AR04
content as site pages; WO03 device detail lives in the electrical-devices
bucket and is only cross-referenced here.

## The art (practical layer)

- **Charge clusters that hold nuclei-sized proton counts are tabulated, not
  merely asserted**: for state parameter α = 3 and relative-emptiness
  β = 10⁻⁷ the cluster retains 2…58 protons with outer radii r₂ = 2.7…14.3
  fermi over n₀ = 2·10³⁰…14·10³⁰ cm⁻³, T = 500…2000 K (nickel nucleus
  ≈ 5 fermi is the stated yardstick) (SC-AR04-029); at α = 10 the same grid
  gives 7…152 protons, r₂ = 7…36 fermi (SC-AR04-030); tables extend the
  self-consistent-field results to nuclear sizes with 1 fermi = 10⁻¹³ cm
  (SC-AR04-028).
- **Shoulders' electron clusters (EV) are the working phenomenology**:
  discovered at a sharp point cathode in vacuum, 1987, applied to metal
  surface processing (SC-AR04-001); sizes fractions to tens of micrometers,
  charge 10⁸–10¹¹ electrons, flash-out (decay) 30–100 ps — longer than
  Coulomb dispersal allows (SC-AR04-002). Appendix-level detail: EV diameter
  ~0.1 µm with ~10¹¹ electrons (minimum 10⁸), an ion-loading limit of
  1 positive ion per 100 000 electrons, speeds ~1/10 c under applied field,
  ~1 µm spheroids and ~20 µm ring chains (SC-MO02-021).
- **NEV / "Plasma injection processing" recipe for nuclear-waste
  transmutation**: EVs enclose ~10⁵ nuclides and absorb ions until the
  charge turns positive (nuclide EV); a patent application by Shoulders
  ("Plasma injection processing") plus parallel filings (Rod Neal, Stan
  Gleeson) are named; a palladium target processed by EV flux yielded
  Mg, Ca, Si, Ga, Au (SC-MO02-023). Reaction kinetics: "loading" weakens
  the material, fast fracture generates an NEV densely loaded with nuclides
  (100 000 deuterons in an electrolytic cell), kV-range applied voltage with
  "megavolt-equivalent" speed from fracture acceleration, reactions run in
  tens of nanoseconds, and radioactivity of highly active material was
  completely eliminated after EV processing (SC-MO02-024) (debt-candidate).
- **Glow-discharge LENR transmutation is repeatable enough to be a
  procedure**: LENR arise when current flows in a glow discharge and show up
  as new elements in the cathode material (SC-AR04-005); Savvatimova's
  cathode analyses were repeated on different gases at different pressures,
  with elements of *smaller* nuclear charge than the cathode substance
  appearing more often, together with local anomalous heat (SC-WO01-082);
  the anomalous heat appears sporadically and is stated to have no
  explanation (SC-AR04-006) (debt-candidate).
- **Fleischmann–Pons cell as reproduced in this corpus**: heavy-water
  electrolysis with platinum and gold electrodes, current on, spontaneous
  excess energy release, explicitly without neutron release (SC-WO01-080);
  a thin plasma layer forms at the palladium electrode that "nobody saw"
  (SC-TC01-045 — electrical-devices bucket); the same corpus elsewhere reports that no neutrons were
  detected in these anomalously heated systems (SC-TC06-037). The author's
  own re-interpretation: charge bubbles enriched the current and the heat
  came from bubble synthesis/decay, not CNF (SC-TC03-005, SC-TC05-005,
  SC-SI10-012) (debt-candidate).
- **E-CAT / Rossi–Focardi solid-plasma heater**: cylindrical conducting
  tube filled with Ni–Li metal chips, charged with hydrogen at 55 bar,
  ~megawatt thermal (SC-TC08-108); Russian repeat by Bazhutov–Koretsky–
  Parkhomov (2012) registered neutron radiation in short bursts and named
  the class "Cold Transmutation of Nuclei" (ХТЯ) (SC-TC08-112); observed
  transmutations move only up the periodic table, Δz = +1: Ni→Cu, Be→B,
  La→Ce, with no downward-moving or decay products found (SC-TC08-113).
- **The discriminator experiment that separates the four candidate heating
  mechanisms**: heat Li–Ni chips with hydrogen removed (vacuum), then with
  varying added hydrogen, and compare thermal output (SC-TC08-139,
  SC-TC08-115); the four directions being (1) author's cold synthesis,
  (2) ХТЯ with neutrons, (3) Sapogin's K-capture cold decay, (4) non-nuclear
  displacement-current mechanisms (SC-TC08-137).
- **Solid-body varicap from chips, hydrogen-free**: place chips of any
  conducting material between two electrodes in vacuum, DC-heat the chip
  points through the electrodes, at critical current an alternating voltage
  appears on the DC terminals, then switch off DC and use the AC source
  (SC-TC08-101).
- **Charge-bubble stages in plasma**: a bubble forms, then expands; in the
  expansion stage Coulomb forces dominate, the bubble radiates and decays,
  radiating more electromagnetic energy than it received "at birth"
  (SC-TC08-033); bubble synthesis/decay in plasma-like media is claimed to
  release energy without any nuclear reactions (SC-TC08-036).
- **EHB (charge bubble) synthesis temperature window**: EHB synthesis
  resembles nucleus synthesis but runs at ~1000 K in plasma instead of the
  ~10⁷ K of nuclear synthesis (SC-TC01-037) (debt-candidate).
- **Energomash (Katorgin) hydrogen-plasma result**: electron and proton
  charge clusters with mean charge 30 µC and ~10¹⁴ carriers each form in
  hydrogen plasma; plasma heats on cluster synthesis/decay; anomalous
  Joule–Lenz effect with electric→thermal conversion coefficient > 2
  (SC-WO01-085); the Katorgin–Marin plasma insert ran at 30 kW with most of
  the displacement current going into harmful unshieldable radiation
  (SC-TC03-027 — discharge-plasma bucket); at the palladium electrode of
  F–P cells the analogous plasma layer was missed (SC-TC01-045 —
  electrical-devices bucket).
- **Proton-cluster charge density targets**: 7–8 mC/nm³ — exceeding
  Shoulders' micron electron clusters by 8·10¹⁷ times, with binding energy
  above all known nuclear binding energies (SC-WO01-027, SC-MO03-005)
  (debt-candidate).
- **Cluster energy balances (the cost/benefit ledger)**: 10¹¹-electron
  cluster, radius 7.5 µm, 10 kV — source work 160 µJ vs 154 mJ stored in
  the charge layer (~10³ ratio), binding energy −18.7 nJ ≈ −1.2·10¹¹ eV,
  i.e. > 10³ × U-235 binding energy; a 1 kW plant would need 5.2·10¹⁰
  cluster decays/s (SC-MO03-010) (debt-candidate); companion estimate:
  radius 15 µm, n₀ = 10²⁰ m⁻³, 2000 K, 1 nC gives ≈ 2 nJ ~ 10¹⁰ eV binding
  energy, three orders above U-235 fission (SC-WO01-052); nucleus-scale
  point model: R = 4·10⁻¹⁵ m, T = 300 K gives α² = 5.7·10⁴, N = 9, layer
  thickness 5.24·10⁻¹⁷ m, Wᵢ = −2.6 eV (SC-MO02-014).
- **Scale law for engineering cluster size**: l = (1/e)·√(ε₀kT/n₀) —
  centimeter clusters at T = 2000 K, n₀ = 1.9·10¹¹ m⁻³; micron at
  n₀ = 1.9·10¹⁹ m⁻³; fermi-scale clusters stop radiating and at
  T ~ 10¹⁰ K reach nuclear mean charge density (SC-WO01-056, SC-MO03-011).
- **Self-charging (Pustovoit–Beletsky) capacitor family**: the device
  recipe, cycle phenomenology, and quantities are bucketed under
  electrical-devices (SC-TC01-029/030/032/033, SC-PM01-011 there; see
  synthesis/electrical-devices.md).
- **Neutron-decay anchor observation**: at neutron liberation the electron
  is pressed to the proton by ~300 N against a 1.44 MeV well, freed by
  charge oscillation of the "trembling string" (SC-AR01-074, SC-SI02-010)
  (debt-candidate) — the wave-mechanical loophole the transmutation
  narratives lean on.
- Historical negative control: a cesium-vapor thermoelement heated by
  neutron reactions in a reactor was built at Los Alamos (1959); per
  Morgulis the recorded efficiency was negligibly small (SC-BR03-031).

## Testable protocols

- **Glow-discharge cathode transmutation scan** (SC-WO01-082, SC-AR04-005,
  SC-AR04-006):
  - Materials: glow-discharge cathode of the metal under test; various
    working gases at various pressures (SC-WO01-082); H₂/He or mixtures
    named as the initiator media (SC-AR04-036).
  - Procedure: 1) run glow-discharge current through the cathode
    (SC-WO01-082); 2) analyze the cathode for transmutation products
    (SC-WO01-082); 3) record local anomalous heat alongside
    (SC-AR04-006, SC-WO01-082).
  - Expected signature per source: new elements, biased toward smaller
    nuclear charge than the cathode (SC-WO01-082). [GAP] no extracted
    current/voltage/pressure values or exposure times for the Savvatimova
    runs.
- **NEV fracture-acceleration waste-processing protocol (Shoulders)**
  (SC-MO02-023, SC-MO02-024, SC-MO02-020):
  - Materials: target material (Pd demonstrated; products Mg, Ca, Si, Ga,
    Au — SC-MO02-023); radioactive waste for the disposal application;
    electrolytic cell for the deuteron variant (100 000 deuterons —
    SC-MO02-024).
  - Geometry: EV ~0.1 µm, ~10¹¹ electrons; NEV carrying ~10⁵ nuclides
    (SC-MO02-021, SC-MO02-023).
  - Procedure: 1) "loading" pass weakening the material structure
    (SC-MO02-024); 2) trigger very fast fracture to generate the NEV
    (SC-MO02-024); 3) accelerate NEV into the receiving material with
    kV-range voltage (fracture supplies MV-equivalent velocity)
    (SC-MO02-024); 4) verify products/thermal signature within tens of ns
    reaction window (SC-MO02-024). [GAP] no fracture mechanism, EV
    generation circuit, or dosimetry extracted.
- **E-CAT-type chips heater + hydrogen-removal discriminator**
  (SC-TC08-108, SC-TC08-115, SC-TC08-139, SC-TC08-137):
  - Materials: Ni–Li metal chips; hydrogen (55 bar in the reference run —
    SC-TC08-108); cylindrical conducting tube; vacuum pump for the
    hydrogen-free arm.
  - Procedure: 1) fill tube with chips, charge with H₂ at 55 bar, heat —
    reference configuration (SC-TC08-108); 2) repeat with chips from which
    hydrogen has been removed, in vacuum; measure the thermal effect
    (SC-TC08-139); 3) add varying hydrogen quantities and repeat, mapping
    the nuclear-mechanism contribution (SC-TC08-139); 4) compare energies
    of the two classes of runs (SC-TC08-115).
  - Decision content: the four candidate mechanisms (SC-TC08-137) predict
    different heat-vs-hydrogen curves; hydrogen removal should *increase*
    heating if synthesis/transmutation is endothermic (SC-TC08-139,
    SC-TC08-114).
- **Solid-body varicap from chips (hydrogen-free)** (SC-TC08-101):
  - Materials: chips of any conducting material; two electrodes; vacuum
    enclosure (absence of hydrogen stated to reduce losses on "Cold Nuclear
    Synthesis" — SC-TC08-101).
  - Procedure: 1) place chips between the electrodes in vacuum
    (SC-TC08-101); 2) DC-heat the chip points through the electrodes to
    maximize conduction-electron concentration (SC-TC08-101); 3) at
    critical current, observe alternating voltage on the DC terminals
    (SC-TC08-101); 4) switch off DC and draw the AC output
    (SC-TC08-101). [GAP] no chip size, material, or critical-current value
    extracted for this exact recipe.
- **Fleischmann–Pons cell (as characterized here)** (SC-WO01-080,
  SC-TC06-037; plasma-layer claim SC-TC01-045 — electrical-devices bucket):
  - Materials: heavy water; platinum and gold electrodes (SC-WO01-080);
    palladium appears as the electrode carrying the active thin liquid
    layer (SC-TC01-045).
  - Procedure: 1) electrolyze heavy water with Pt/Au electrodes
    (SC-WO01-080); 2) pass current and monitor for spontaneous excess
    energy (SC-WO01-080); 3) probe the thin liquid layer at the Pd
    electrode for plasma (SC-TC01-045); 4) run neutron diagnostics — the
    source class reports heating without neutrons (SC-TC06-037).
    [GAP] no current densities, cell geometry, or calorimetry protocol
    extracted in this bucket.

## Phenomenology map

- **Transmutation in discharges**: new elements appear in glow-discharge
  cathodes, biased to smaller z, reproducible across gases and pressures,
  with sporadic local anomalous heat (SC-AR04-005, SC-AR04-006,
  SC-WO01-082).
- **Transmutation in solid chips**: ХТЯ moves nuclei up the table by exactly
  one charge unit (Ni→Cu, Be→B, La→Ce); neutron flux appears in short
  bursts; downward-moving nuclei and decay products are absent
  (SC-TC08-112, SC-TC08-113); heat output of ХТЯ is asserted both present
  (SC-TC08-113) and absent/endothermic (SC-TC08-114) — an internal conflict
  for future work.
- **Anomalous heat without neutrons**: F–P heavy-water electrolysis
  (SC-WO01-080, SC-TC06-037); Energomash hydrogen plasma with conversion
  coefficient > 2 (SC-WO01-085); the corpus's own bubble reinterpretation
  of all of these (SC-TC03-005, SC-TC06-047, SC-SI10-012, SC-TC02-038).
- **EV/NEV effects**: micron clusters survive 30–100 ps against Coulomb
  dispersal (SC-AR04-002); EV flux processes targets producing new
  substances and eliminating radioactivity (SC-MO02-020, SC-MO02-023,
  SC-MO02-024).
- **Scale/temperature ladder**: bubble synthesis at ~1000 K vs nuclear
  synthesis at ~10⁷ K (SC-TC01-037); fermi-scale clusters non-radiating
  with nuclear-density charge at T ~ 10¹⁰ K (SC-WO01-056); nuclei modeled
  as the smallest thin-wall bubbles at T ~ 10¹¹ K (SC-SI01-016,
  SC-WO04-026).
- **Cross-bucket coupling**: the same bubbles are the "reactor" of the
  displacement-current devices (see synthesis/electrical-devices.md:
  SC-TC02-014, SC-SI01-018), and the nanofilm explosion thresholds
  (SC-TC04-014, in synthesis/discharge-plasma.md) bound the chip
  technologies (SC-TC08-101) by the current density they can survive.

## Theory aside (secondary)

The load-bearing model is the self-consistent-field gasostatics of AR04:
equilibrium E + f = 0 with div E = 4πρ, Boltzmann closure giving the Emden
class equation Δφ = −4πρ₀exp(∓eφ/kT), the exact plane solution
y = ±ln(cos ξ), an approximate α ≥ 3 hollow-cluster solution whose sine
zeros define inner/outer boundaries r₁, r₂, logarithmic concentration
singularities n/n₀ = R²/(α²δ²), and particle counts N/N* ~ (α/2β)[(r₁/R)² +
(r₂/R)²] (SC-AR04-008 through SC-AR04-026). Everything experimental above is
read through this lens: proton-cluster tables (SC-AR04-029, SC-AR04-030 —
debt-candidates), the β = 10⁻⁶ fit to Shoulders' clusters (SC-AR04-027), and
the nucleus-as-bubble model (SC-AR08-006, SC-MO03-004, SC-WO01-025 —
debt-candidates). A second model strand — the wave-packet charge whose
oscillation lets total energy grow or shrink in a potential well (SC-TC02-064,
SC-TC08-131, SC-WO03-020) — supplies the "maternity ward / crematorium"
trajectory classes and the K-capture cold-decay narrative (SC-TC08-132,
SC-TC08-135, SC-TC08-136). None of this is load-bearing for the practical
layer: the protocols above can be run and falsified without accepting the
gasostatic retention mechanism (flagged debt-candidate at SC-AR04-012) or
the binding-energy estimates (SC-SI01-017, SC-SI04-004). Debt-candidate
registry: clusters/debt-candidates.md (AR04, MO02, MO03, TC01, TC02, TC06,
TC08, WO01, WO03, WO04 entries).
